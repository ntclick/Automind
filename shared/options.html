Live Captions

Two steps run back to back: speech is transcribed (ASR), then the text is translated into your subtitle language. Reading subtitles aloud (TTS) uses Chrome's built-in voices — no key needed. Keys for these engines live on the API Keys tab.

Chrome transcribes locally through your microphone. No key and no cost, but it listens to the mic rather than the tab, so play the audio out loud and expect lower accuracy on noisy streams.
Google Translate never touches your 50/day allowance. Premium AI reads the surrounding conversation, so it handles slang and tech terms far better — recommended for crypto/tech streams.

🎬
Video TLDR → Post

Reads the whole video through Whisper, then boils its main points down to one ready-to-post summary — easy to understand, concise, in the language you pick. Everything runs from the panel's TLDR tab: Auto-read fetches and transcribes the post's video hidden, in seconds, no playback; Read video is the realtime fallback for livestreams. Keys live on the API Keys tab.

"Same as Reply Language" follows the Reply Style tab; if that is Auto, the post matches the video's spoken language.

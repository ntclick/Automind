## Description

AutoMind is a powerful dual-purpose browser assistant for X.com (Twitter) that helps you reply smarter and follow live audio effortlessly.

**AI Smart Replies for X.com**
Generate context-aware, high-quality replies for any X post with a single click. Choose from multiple tone styles — Professional, Casual, Witty, Concise, Sarcastic, or Analytical — to match your voice. The extension automatically detects the post language and drafts replies accordingly.

**Live Audio Translation & Real-Time Subtitles**
Capture active tab audio from X Spaces, live streams, or videos and display real-time translated subtitles in a dedicated Side Panel. Subtitles appear alongside your timeline so you never miss a word. Our latest engine renders captions faster and paces them naturally with the speaker, so you're not left waiting for text to catch up. Translations read fluently instead of sounding machine-translated, and smarter filtering keeps stray transcription noise off your screen. An optional Text-to-Speech readout lets you listen to translated captions hands-free.

**Free Daily Tier + Bring Your Own Key**
Enjoy 50 free operations per day out of the box — no setup required. Need unlimited usage? Connect your own API key or link to a local AI server from the settings page.

**Privacy First**
All settings and session data are stored locally in your browser. No personal data or browsing history is ever collected or shared.

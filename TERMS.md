## 🎯 2. Description of Service

AutoMind is a productivity browser extension designed for the X (Twitter.com) platform that provides:
* **AI-Powered Replies**: Automatically generates contextual draft replies for X posts in multiple tones (including Web3/Crypto analytical styles).
* **Live Speech Captions & Translation**: Captures active tab audio (such as live X Spaces, YouTube videos, and podcasts) and generates real-time translated subtitles presented in a docked Side Panel.
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice Readout**: Provides optional voice audio output of translated captions for accessibility.
* **Multiple AI Model Integrations**: Connects to various third-party AI platforms (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq, Kimi, DeepSeek, and Local APIs).
